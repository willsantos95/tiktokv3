Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026
Last Updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Relampago de Ofertas, operated by Relampago de Ofertas, collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when users connect their TikTok accounts and use the application’s video publishing features.

1. Information We Collect

TikTok account information

  • TikTok account identifier;
  • Display name or username;
  • Profile information made available through the authorized TikTok API;
  • Authentication and authorization tokens;
  • Information required to confirm that the account is connected.

Video and publication information

  • Videos selected by the user for publication;
  • Video titles, descriptions, captions and hashtags;
  • Publication status;
  • TikTok post or publication identifiers;
  • Date and time of publication;
  • Technical responses returned by TikTok’s API.

Technical information

  • Application logs and error messages;
  • Authentication events;
  • Request dates and times;
  • Browser, device or IP information when necessary for security and operation;
  • Information about failed or completed publication requests.

2. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you authorize Relampago de Ofertas through TikTok, select a video, request publication, receive a response from TikTok, use the application or contact support.

Relampago de Ofertas does not request or store your TikTok password.

3. How We Use Information

  • Connecting and authenticating your TikTok account;
  • Uploading and publishing videos requested by the user;
  • Managing and displaying publication status;
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized access;
  • Diagnosing errors and providing technical support;
  • Maintaining application security and operation;
  • Complying with legal obligations and TikTok platform requirements.

Relampago de Ofertas does not publish content without user authorization.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, information is processed based on consent, performance of the service requested, legitimate security interests, and compliance with legal or platform obligations.

5. TikTok API Data

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide features authorized by the user and is handled according to applicable TikTok developer terms, policies and permission requirements.

Access tokens and related credentials are treated as confidential information and used only to communicate with authorized TikTok services.

6. Sharing of Information

Relampago de Ofertas does not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information may be shared only with TikTok, necessary infrastructure providers, authorities when legally required, or professional advisers when necessary to protect legal rights.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application, process publications, resolve errors, comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

Uploaded video files may be deleted after publication or after the processing period. Authentication tokens are deleted, invalidated or no longer used after disconnection, expiration or revocation.

8. Data Security

Relampago de Ofertas uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS, access controls, restricted token access, secure hosting, monitoring and secret management.

No online system is completely secure, and absolute protection cannot be guaranteed.

9. User Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, including Brazil’s General Data Protection Law, users may request confirmation, access, correction, deletion, revocation of consent, information about data sharing and portability when legally applicable.

10. Revoking TikTok Access

Users may revoke access through TikTok account settings. After revocation, Relampago de Ofertas will no longer access new TikTok information or publish new videos. Previously published content may remain on TikTok.

11. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information associated with your account, email contato@relampagodeoferta.shop. Include enough information to identify the connected account. Do not send passwords, access tokens or other sensitive credentials.

12. Children’s Privacy

The application is not directed to children under 13 or under the minimum age required by TikTok in the user’s country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

13. International Data Processing

Hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. Where required, reasonable safeguards will be used for international transfers.

14. Third-Party Services

The application may link to TikTok or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, legal requirements or TikTok platform requirements change. The updated version will display a revised date.
